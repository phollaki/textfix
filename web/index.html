Questions

Do you store my text?

No. Your text goes to our server and to OpenAI only to create the new version. We don't save it and we don't log it. We only count how many rewrites you use each month. Read the privacy policy.

Why does TextFix need Accessibility access?

TextFix presses Copy and Paste for you. macOS only allows this for apps with Accessibility access. TextFix does nothing until you press one of its shortcuts.

Which apps does it work in?

In every app where you can select text and use Copy and Paste: Mail, Slack, Teams, Notes, Pages, Word, Safari, Chrome and more.

How long can the text be?

Up to 4,000 characters at a time. That's about a page and a half.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro is an App Store subscription. Cancel it any time in the App Store under your account settings, or with "Manage subscription" in TextFix.
