Questions

Do you store my text?

No. There is no TextFix server. Your Mac sends the text directly to the AI provider you pick, with your own key.

Where do I get an API key?

From your provider's website, for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google AI Studio. Add a few dollars of credit, then paste the key into the TextFix settings. The settings have a direct link for every provider.

Can I use it without the cloud?

Yes. Install Ollama, download a model, and choose "Ollama (on this Mac)" in the settings. Your text never leaves your Mac.

macOS says it cannot check TextFix for malware

TextFix is not notarized by Apple yet. Close the message, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to TextFix. You only do this once. You can also read and build the source code yourself.

Why does TextFix need Accessibility access?

TextFix presses Copy and Paste for you. macOS only allows this for apps with Accessibility access. TextFix does nothing until you press one of its shortcuts.

Which apps does it work in?

In every app where you can select text and use Copy and Paste: Mail, Slack, Teams, Notes, Pages, Word, Safari, Chrome and more.
