Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

TextFix is a Mac app that rewrites the text you select. This page explains which data TextFix uses and why.

The text you select

When you press a TextFix shortcut, the app sends the selected text over an encrypted connection to the TextFix server. The server sends it to OpenAI to create the new version and sends the result back to your Mac.

We don't store your text and we don't write it to our logs. OpenAI processes the text as our service provider. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models and may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse. See the OpenAI API data usage policy.

Usage data

TextFix creates a random ID when you first open it. The ID is not linked to your name, email address or device. The server stores this ID with the number of rewrites you used each month, so it can apply the free limit.

Purchases

Apple handles all payments. If you subscribe to Pro, the app sends the signed App Store transaction to our server to check that the subscription is active. We store the transaction ID with your monthly rewrite count. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

What we don't collect

No account, no email address, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites.

Data storage and deletion

Monthly usage counts are deleted after 13 months. To delete your data sooner, write to us with the ID shown at the bottom of TextFix settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your data, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.
