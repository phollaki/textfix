Support

Something doesn't work? Write to textfix@puteanihollakos.com. We usually answer within two working days.

The shortcut does nothing

Open TextFix from your Applications folder to see its settings. If it says that TextFix needs Accessibility access, click "Open Accessibility Settings", turn on TextFix, then click "Restart TextFix".

TextFix says the shortcut is used by another app

Another app already uses that key combination. In TextFix settings, click the shortcut and press a different one.

The new text is not pasted

TextFix only pastes into the app where you selected the text. If you switch apps while it works, the new text stays in your clipboard and you can paste it yourself with ⌘V.

I used all my rewrites

The free plan has 30 rewrites a month and Pro has 2,000. Your count resets on the 1st of every month. You can upgrade in TextFix settings.

I bought Pro but TextFix still shows Free

Open TextFix settings and click "Restore purchases". Make sure you use the same Apple ID that you used for the purchase.

Cancel or change your subscription

Open the App Store, click your name, then "Account Settings" and "Manage" next to Subscriptions. Or use "Manage subscription" in TextFix settings.
